I report investigative stories using data: public records, scraped websites, leaked lists, etc. I chase stories hidden within data in plain sight.

I was the head editor of The Daily Californian’s Data department in 2026 and was its deputy editor before that. Because I quickly became an editor, much of my work runs under other people’s bylines; the notes below say what I contributed on each piece. I study Economics and Political Economy at UC Berkeley and am spending fall 2026 at the University of Alicante.
